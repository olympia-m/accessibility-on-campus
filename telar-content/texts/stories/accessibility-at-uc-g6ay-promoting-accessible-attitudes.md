---
title: "Promoting Accessible Attitudes"
---

The ADA Advisory Committee also recognized that accessibility depended on creating an environment where disabled students could fully participate in the social aspects of university life. This is reflected in the UCSB ADA Faculty Handbook, which served as a guide for faculty and staff members on implementing ADA requirements in the classroom while also remaining respectful during daily interactions with students. The handbook defines "program accessibility" as providing students with accommodations to ensure equal opportunity and support their success. This resource outlines how staff members can utilize proper etiquette, such as avoiding antiquated terms when referencing a person's disability, as they communicate and accommodate a student's needs. Creating a supportive environment helps students feel comfortable requesting available resources.
