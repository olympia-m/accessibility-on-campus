---
title: "Promoting Accessible Attitudes"
---

The ADA Advisory Committee also recognized that accessibility required an environment where disabled students could fully participate in the social aspects of university life. This idea is reflected in the UCSB ADA Faculty Handbook, which guided faculty and staff in implementing ADA requirements in the classroom while maintaining respectful interactions with students. The handbook defines "program accessibility" as providing accommodations that ensure equal opportunity and support student success. It also outlines proper etiquette, such as avoiding outdated terms when referring to a person's disability, to help staff communicate effectively and accommodate students' needs. By creating a supportive environment, it helps students feel comfortable requesting available resources.
