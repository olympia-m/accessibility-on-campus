---
title: "Hugh Marsh (1934-2012)"
---

Chester Hugh Marsh was born in 1934, and at age 14 was stricken with [[polio]] that resulted in him navigating life in a wheelchair. Before arriving to Santa Barbara, Marsh was a disability advocate and involved in California Governor Jerry Brown's first administration as a publicist. In Santa Barbara, he was a coordinator at the Independent Living Resource Center before becoming a faculty member for UCSB's Engineering Department in 1983.
