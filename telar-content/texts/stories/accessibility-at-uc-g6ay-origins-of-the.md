---
title: "Origins of the Disabled Students Program"
---

The Special Services Program was established in 1978 as one of the first campus support networks for students with disabilities. At its founding, it served about 125 UCSB students and offered programs such as counseling, class pre-registration, interpretive services, and hearing assistive technology. Now known as the Disabled Students Program, it has grown significantly and supports more than 3,000 students each year.
