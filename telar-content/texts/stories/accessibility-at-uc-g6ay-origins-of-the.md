---
title: "Origins of the Disabled Students Program"
---

The Special Services Program was established in 1978 and was one of the first institutional support networks meant to assist disabled students on campus. At its founding, the SSP served around 125 UCSB students, and provided students with programs such as counseling, class pre-registration, interpretive services, and hearing assistive technology. Now known as the Disabled Students Program, the service has expanded dramatically and now assists more than 3,000 students on campus each year.
