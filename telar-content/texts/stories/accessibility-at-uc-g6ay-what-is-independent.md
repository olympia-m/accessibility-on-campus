---
title: "What is Independent Living?"
---

Independent Living is a philosophy developed by disability activists in the late 1960s and early 1970s. Centered on self-determination and deinstitutionalization, it holds that disabled individuals know their own needs best and should have control over their lives. This philosophy arose from a history in which people with disabilities had little control, often forced to live at home with family or in institutions, where reliance on others limited their autonomy.
