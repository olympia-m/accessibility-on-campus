---
title: "What is Independent Living?"
---

- Independent Living is a philosophy developed by disability activists in the late 1960s and early 1970s. Focusing on self-determination and deinstitutionalization, Independent Living is the idea that disabled individuals are the most knowledgeable as to what their own needs are, and as a result should be able to control over their own lives. These ideas came from a legacy of disabled individuals having little control over their own lives, often forced to live at home with family members or in an institutional center. These settings forced people with disabilities to rely on others, depriving them of their autonomy.
