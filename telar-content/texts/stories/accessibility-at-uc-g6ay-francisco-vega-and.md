---
title: "Francisco Vega and the Mobility Transport Program"
---

Francisco Vega was a UCSB transfer student who arrived to campus in Fall 2022. Born with spina bifida, which impacted his ability to easily get to class, he looked into an accessible way to get to-and-from campus. With no campus-established service available, he coordinated with the Veterans and Military Services to form a golf cart service that would assist in transporting those with temporary and permanent physical disabilities. With a recent funding initiative passed in the 2026 Associated Students Election, MTP can only continue to expand its services and help more campus community members.
