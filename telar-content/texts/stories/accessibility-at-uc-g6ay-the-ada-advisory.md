---
title: "The ADA Advisory Committee"
---

Under the direction of Vice Chancellor of Administrative Affairs David Sheldon, the ADA Advisory Committee was established and composed of members of the campus community. The committee included twenty board members from a wide range of departments, including Administrative Services, Environmental Health and Safety, Housing and Residential Services, and the campus fire marshal, among others. While the committee was responsible for formally incorporating accessibility into campus policies and infrastructure, feedback and recommendations from more than two hundred community members across administrative and academic departments helped identify where changes were needed.
